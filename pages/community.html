Our Community

We are committed to giving back to the communities where we live, work, and play. Through our global brands and local teams, we aim to reach 10 million people by 2030.

Do More ambition

  • Reach 10 million people by end of 2030
  • Focus on sport, technology, and wellbeing
  • Enable local action through global scale
  • Track measurable outcomes yearly

Working with communities

Do More is how we give back.

Our brands are close to customers and communities, so they are well placed to identify local needs and respond with relevant action — from health awareness campaigns to youth entrepreneurship programs and digital literacy initiatives.
