Sustainability

Responsible Gaming

As one of the leading names in online sports betting and gaming, FevoTech is committed to positive play. Responsible gaming is essential to long-term customer trust and sustainable business performance.

Read Sustainability Plan Community Actions

Play Well 2030 Goal

  • 75% of online customers using at least one safety tool
  • Smarter interventions powered by behavioral data
  • Consistent support across all key markets
  • Continuous innovation and research investment

Play Well principles

Four principles guiding every market.

Lead Progress

Invest in research, innovation, and collaboration to define and improve positive play.

Promote Positive Play

Design seamless tools and experiences that help customers stay in control while they play.

Effective Interaction

Use data and personalized journeys to prompt reflection when risk signals are detected.

Support and Protect

Provide stronger intervention pathways, trained teams, and partnerships for targeted support.

Making a difference

Measured progress and investment.

$150m investment

FevoTech increased annual investment in responsible gaming research, education, and product protections to accelerate customer safety outcomes.

Scale impact through shared knowledge

Teams share proven interventions and product learnings across brands to improve impact quickly in multiple regions.
