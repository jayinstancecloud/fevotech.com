Sustainability

Our Sustainability

FevoTech is building for the long term. Our sustainability approach is designed to support customers, colleagues, communities, and the environment through measurable action.

Responsible Gaming

Game plan for positive change

  • Play Well for customers
  • Work Better for colleagues
  • Do More for communities
  • Go Zero for climate impact

Positive Impact Plan

Four priorities guiding our sustainability strategy.

Play Well

Improve tools and education so customers can pause, reflect, and make informed choices.

Work Better

Build a safe, supportive, and inclusive culture where colleagues can thrive.

Do More

Work with communities through programs focused on sport, technology, and wellbeing.

Go Zero

Target net zero emissions by 2035 and contribute to broader climate transition efforts.

Local action

A global company delivering local impact.

While our sustainability priorities are global, execution is local. FevoTech brands respond dynamically to market needs, regulations, and cultural expectations to deliver relevant impact.
