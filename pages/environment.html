Our Environment

FevoTech is committed to reaching net zero emissions by 2035. As we grow, we are taking practical action to reduce our environmental impact across operations, supply chain, and long-term climate initiatives.

Tackling climate change

Acting now for a better tomorrow.

FevoTech is implementing a three-part climate approach covering emissions reduction, residual carbon management, and support for broader transition projects that deliver social and environmental value.

Three-part approach

How we plan to reach our target.

Reducing emissions

Lower direct and indirect emissions through efficiency, cleaner energy, and supplier action.

Removing emissions

Develop a credible strategy for durable, high-integrity removals for residual emissions.

Supporting transition

Back projects beyond our value chain that accelerate wider environmental progress.

2025 progress

Measured progress indicators.
