Launching new player protection controls across major markets

Apr 22, 2026 | Responsible Gaming

FevoTech has rolled out upgraded player protection controls across multiple regions, giving customers clearer tools to manage spend, session time, and account limits.

Safer play by design

The latest release introduces a redesigned control center where players can activate deposit limits, timeout periods, and notification reminders from one place. The update makes key actions faster, while preserving clear explanations of each setting.

Smarter risk signals

FevoTech also improved machine-learning signals that identify rapid changes in behavior. When thresholds are met, in-product messaging now encourages players to review limits before continuing.

Operator and customer impact

Early results show stronger use of customer controls and faster support response times. The company plans to continue introducing region-specific safer-gaming features over the next two quarters.
