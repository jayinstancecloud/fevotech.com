Partnering with startups to accelerate accessibility in sports tech

Apr 04, 2026 | Partnerships

FevoTech is partnering with early-stage companies building accessibility-first features that make digital sports experiences more inclusive.

Supporting inclusive product design

The partnership program will fund pilot projects focused on screen-reader compatibility, simplified interaction patterns, and adaptive interface controls for diverse player needs.

From pilot to platform

Selected startups will test solutions in controlled environments before expanding into broader product areas. The goal is to rapidly validate impact and integrate winning ideas.

Shared long-term vision

By combining startup agility with FevoTech distribution and domain expertise, the program aims to improve accessibility standards across the wider sports technology ecosystem.
