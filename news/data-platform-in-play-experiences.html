How our data platform improved in-play customer experiences

Apr 15, 2026 | Technology

A series of platform upgrades reduced decision latency and improved reliability during peak events, delivering smoother in-play experiences for customers.

Faster event pipelines

FevoTech migrated key event processing services to a lower-latency architecture. This improved data freshness and reduced price update delays during high-volume matches.

Resilience during traffic spikes

The engineering team introduced automated scaling policies and tighter service isolation. During recent tournament weeks, the platform sustained significantly higher throughput without customer-facing degradation.

Personalization at scale

With cleaner real-time signals, recommendation models now adapt faster to customer intent. This improved discovery across in-play markets while maintaining responsible experience guardrails.
